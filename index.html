Want a harder challenge? Follow this challenge I made up with my friends

  • You and your friends can only use 3 rerolls on the loadout, or 3 rerolls on weapons, tools or consumables.
  • It's 3 rerolls as a team, not individually. Whatever comes out in the loadout is what the whole team takes.
  • You can't swap weapons during the match. You can only swap weapons if your objective as a solo or as a team is to extract.
  • You can't swap ammo during the match (you can use boxes to reload ammo).
  • You can only pick up tools and consumables during the match.
  • If you extract successfully, whatever you carry in your inventory is what you'll use in the next game. If you don't extract with all your tools or consumables slots filled, you can only reroll the slots you have empty.
  • If you die during the match, start this challenge over.
